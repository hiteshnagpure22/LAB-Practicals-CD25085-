Tribute to Ratan Tata

Ratan Tata (28 December 1937 – 9 October 2024) was a visionary Indian industrialist, philanthropist, and former Chairman of Tata Sons. He led the Tata Group from 1991 to 2012, transforming it into a global conglomerate with major acquisitions like Jaguar Land Rover and Tetley. Known for his ethical leadership and modesty, he significantly advanced philanthropy through Tata Trusts.

Achievements
  • Global Expansion & Acquisitions: He led the acquisition of international giants, including Tetley, Corus Steel, and Jaguar Land Rover, positioning Tata as a global brand
  • Massive Group Growth: Under his leadership, Tata Group revenues grew from roughly $5.8 billion in 1991 to over $85 billion by 2012.
  • Innovation (Tata Nano): He conceptualized the Tata Nano, aimed at providing an affordable and safe transportation option for middle-class Indian families.
  • Philanthropy and Social Impact: Through Tata Trusts, he significantly supported health care, education, and rural development, with a major focus on cancer research centers.
